Frequently Asked

What are house covering nets?
House covering nets are protective nets installed around houses, buildings, balconies, and construction areas to help provide protection from dust, debris, falling objects, and unwanted access during construction or renovation work.
Why are house covering nets used?
House covering nets are commonly used during construction, renovation, painting, and repair work to help control dust and debris while providing an additional layer of protection around the property.
Where can house covering nets be installed?
House covering nets can be installed around villas, apartments, independent houses, residential buildings, construction sites, balconies, terraces, and other areas where protective netting is required.
Do house covering nets help reduce dust?
Yes. Properly installed house covering nets can help reduce the spread of dust and lightweight debris from construction, renovation, painting, and other building activities.
Are house covering nets suitable for construction sites?
Yes. House covering nets can be used around residential construction and renovation sites to provide an additional protective barrier and help contain dust and small debris.

Can house covering nets be used during renovation?
