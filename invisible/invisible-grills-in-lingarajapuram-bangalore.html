What are invisible grills?
Invisible grills are thin, high-strength stainless steel cables installed on balconies and windows to provide an additional safety barrier while maintaining an open and unobstructed view. They are a modern alternative to traditional heavy metal grills.
Are invisible grills safe for children and pets?
Professionally installed invisible grills can provide an additional safety barrier for balconies and windows. They can be useful for homes with children and pets while maintaining visibility and ventilation.
What material is used for invisible grills?
Simon Invisible Grills uses stainless steel invisible grill cables along with suitable fittings and tensioning components for balcony and window installations. Material specifications can be selected according to the installation requirements.

Yes. Invisible Grills in Lingarajapuram can be installed on apartment and villa balconies. Their slim cable design helps maintain the appearance and outdoor view of the balcony while providing an additional safety barrier.

How long does invisible grill installation take?
