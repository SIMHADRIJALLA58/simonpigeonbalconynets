Frequently Asked

What are balcony safety nets?
Balcony safety nets are UV-treated HDPE nets fitted to apartment balconies to prevent falls of children, pets and objects while remaining nearly invisible.
Are invisible grills safe?
Yes — our invisible grills use 316-grade stainless steel cables tensioned to withstand over 250 kg of horizontal load with clamps engineered for high-rise buildings.
How long does installation take?
Most balcony & pigeon net jobs finish in 2–3 hours. Invisible grills usually take 4–6 hours per balcony.
Do pigeon nets harm birds?
No. Our pigeon nets are humane physical barriers — they deter birds without causing injury.
What is the warranty?
All installations carry a 1-year replacement warranty. Nets themselves are UV-warranted up to 5–8 years depending on grade.
How much do safety nets cost?
Pricing starts at ₹10/sq.ft for standard pigeon nets. Request a free on-site inspection for an accurate quote.
Do you provide installation across Bengaluru?
Yes — we cover all Bengaluru zones including HSR, Koramangala, Whitefield, Electronic City, Yelahanka, Kengeri and more.
Is mosquito net removable?
Yes, our Velcro & sliding mosquito nets are fully removable for washing and seasonal storage.
Can children damage the net?
Our children-grade nets use tightly woven high-tensile mesh tested to withstand repeated impact from active children.
