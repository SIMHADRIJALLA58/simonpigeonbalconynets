Frequently Asked

What are cloth hangers?
Cloth hangers are devices used to hang and organize clothes on a rack or wall, helping to keep them neat and wrinkle-free.
Are wooden hangers better than plastic?
Wooden hangers are more durable and provide better support for heavier garments, while plastic hangers are lighter and more affordable.
How many hangers do I need?
The number of hangers you need depends on your wardrobe size and storage space. A good starting point is one hanger per outfit.
Can I use regular hangers for all types of clothes?
Regular hangers work well for most clothing items, but some delicate or structured garments may require specialized hangers for optimal support.
What is the best material for hangers?
The best material depends on your needs. Wood offers durability, metal provides strength, and plastic is lightweight and cost-effective.
How do I choose the right size hanger?
Choose a hanger size that accommodates your clothing items without causing distortion or damage. Measure your garments to ensure proper fit.
Do you provide installation across Bengaluru?
Yes — we cover all Bengaluru zones including HSR, Koramangala, Whitefield, Electronic City, Yelahanka, Kengeri and more.
Is mosquito net removable?
Yes, our Velcro & sliding mosquito nets are fully removable for washing and seasonal storage.
Can children damage the net?
Our children-grade nets use tightly woven high-tensile mesh tested to withstand repeated impact from active children.
