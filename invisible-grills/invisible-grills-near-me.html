    Frequently Asked

    What are invisible grills?
    Invisible grills are thin, high-strength stainless steel cables installed on balconies and windows to provide safety while maintaining an open and unobstructed view. They offer a modern and minimal alternative to traditional metal grills.
    Are invisible grills safe for children and pets?
    Yes. Professionally installed invisible grills provide an additional safety barrier for balconies and windows, helping improve safety for children and pets while maintaining the view.
    What material is used for invisible grills?
    Our invisible grills use high-quality stainless steel safety cables with durable fittings designed for strength, weather resistance, and long-lasting performance.
    Are invisible grills suitable for balconies?
    Yes. Invisible grills are ideal for apartment and villa balconies. They provide a strong safety barrier without significantly affecting the outside view or the appearance of your balcony.
    Can invisible grills be installed on windows?
    Yes. Invisible grills can be installed on different types of windows to improve safety and prevent accidental falls while maintaining ventilation, natural light, and visibility.
    How much do invisible grills cost in Bengaluru?
    The cost of invisible grills depends on the size of the balcony or window, the number of cables required, the fittings, and installation requirements. Contact Simon Invisible Grills for an accurate and affordable quote based on your measurements.
    How long does invisible grill installation take?
    Installation time depends on the size and complexity of the balcony or window. Most standard installations can be completed within a few hours after proper measurement and preparation.
    Do invisible grills block the view?
    No. One of the main advantages of invisible grills is their minimal appearance. The thin stainless steel cables are designed to provide safety while keeping your outside view largely unobstructed.
    Are invisible grills weather resistant?
    Yes. Our invisible grill systems use durable stainless steel cables and fittings designed to withstand normal outdoor conditions, making them suitable for balconies and windows.
    How can I book invisible grill installation in Bengaluru?
    You can contact Simon Invisible Grills to schedule a site inspection and measurement. Our team will understand your requirements, recommend a suitable invisible grill solution, and provide a quotation.
