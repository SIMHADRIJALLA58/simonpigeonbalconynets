Frequently Asked Questions

What are Velcro mosquito nets?
Velcro mosquito nets are removable insect screens fitted around windows or doors using Velcro strips. They help prevent mosquitoes and other insects from entering while allowing fresh air and natural light into your home.
Are Velcro mosquito nets removable?
Yes. Velcro mosquito nets are designed to be easily removable for cleaning, washing, or storage. They can also be refitted when required.
Where can mosquito nets be installed?
Mosquito nets can be installed on windows, doors, balconies, bedrooms, kitchens, utility areas, and other openings where mosquito and insect protection is required.
Are Velcro mosquito nets suitable for apartments?
Yes. Velcro mosquito nets are a practical option for apartments, villas, independent houses, and other residential properties. They can be customized according to the size and design of your windows and doors.
How long does mosquito net installation take?
Installation time depends on the number and size of windows or doors. Most standard mosquito net installations can be completed within a few hours after accurate measurement and preparation.
Do mosquito nets allow fresh air and sunlight?
Yes. Quality mosquito net mesh allows air and natural light to pass through while creating a physical barrier against mosquitoes, flies, and other insects.
