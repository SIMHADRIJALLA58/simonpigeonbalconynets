Frequently Asked Questions

Why are pigeon spikes used on balconies and ledges?
Pigeon Spikes are designed to discourage pigeons from landing on suitable surfaces such as balcony ledges, parapets, window edges, AC outdoor units, and other commonly used landing points. They can help reduce repeated bird activity in these areas.
Where can pigeon nets be installed in Electronic City Phase 2?
Pigeon Nets in Electronic City Phase 2 can be installed across balconies, windows, ducts, shafts, utility areas, and other larger openings where pigeons may enter or build nests. The net arrangement is planned according to the dimensions and structure of the opening.
What is the difference between pigeon spikes and pigeon nets?
Pigeon Spikes are generally used on exposed landing surfaces such as ledges and parapets, while Pigeon Nets are used to cover open spaces and prevent birds from entering an area. Simon Pigeon Spikes and Nets can assess the location and suggest a suitable option based on the property's structure.
Can pigeon spikes be installed on apartment buildings?
Yes. Pigeon Spikes for Apartments in Electronic City Phase 2 can be fitted on suitable balcony edges, window ledges, parapets, and other accessible surfaces. The installation method depends on the surface condition and available fixing points.
Can pigeon nets cover an entire balcony?

Do pigeon droppings create hygiene and cleaning problems?
Pigeon droppings, feathers, and nesting materials can make balconies, ledges, and other areas difficult to keep clean. Regular bird activity may also create unwanted hygiene concerns. Pigeon Spikes & Nets can help reduce bird landing and entry in commonly affected areas.
Are pigeon spikes harmful to birds?
Properly selected and installed bird spikes are intended as a deterrent rather than a trapping system. Their purpose is to make a landing surface unsuitable for pigeons so they are encouraged to use another location.
